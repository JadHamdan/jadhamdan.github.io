Jad Hamdan

hamdan[at]maths.ox.ac.uk

I completed my DPhil under Jon Keating at Oxford (the Queen's College). I'm broadly interested in random matrix theory, analytic number theory, Gaussian multiplicative chaos, and their interactions.

I also organise the Oxford Random Matrix Theory Seminar.
